How to play

Ten colour pairs. For each one, say how far it carries.

  • Body copy

    4.5:1 or better. Safe at any size.

  • Large only

    3:1 to 4.5:1. Fine at 24px, or 19px bold. Not for body text.

  • Nowhere

    Under 3:1. Fails even for large text and interface parts.

Those three numbers are the WCAG AA thresholds. Most pairs you'll see sit near a boundary, because that's where the judgement is actually hard.

Contrast is a luminance calculation, so hue barely moves it. Two colours can look completely different and still fail.

Switch the theme mid-set and watch a pair you have already judged. It will look different against a dark page than a light one. The ratio has not moved at all — your eyes have. That gap is the reason the number exists.
