      How to play

      A color is hidden. Match it in six tries.

      Drag the three sliders to mix a color. H is hue, the color itself. S is saturation, grey through to vivid. L is lightness, black through to white.

      Each try scores the three sliders separately:

      • H 212✓

        Matched. Leave this slider alone.

      • S 64↑

        Close. Nudge it higher.

      • L 20↓

        Way off. Drag it a long way down.

      The arrow always points toward the answer. You win when all three are matched on the same try.

      Finish a round and another color is waiting. Play as many as you like. Everyone gets the same colors in the same order each day, so scores compare. The number beside the title is today's puzzle, counting up one a day.
